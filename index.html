What does Forge Consultancy do?

We set up private AI operations for businesses in India. We install an AI chief of staff on your own laptops that reads email and WhatsApp, tracks collections and compliance deadlines, drafts replies and tells each person only what needs them. We also build software for product teams abroad.

Who sets up a private AI assistant for a business in Delhi NCR or Haryana?

Forge Consultancy, based in Bahadurgarh, Haryana. We work with business owners and leadership teams across Delhi NCR, Haryana and Punjab, and remotely across India.

Is our company data sent to Forge or to a third-party app?

No. The assistant runs on your own laptops. We do not host or store your email, WhatsApp, documents or bank details. The AI model is used through your own subscription, and records are kept as plain files you own.

Can the AI send emails or payments on its own?

No. It reads, researches and drafts. A person approves and sends every message, payment and filing.

How is this different from an off-the-shelf AI assistant app?

Off-the-shelf apps keep your data in their cloud, need you to set them up, are generic and are hard to leave. We set it up for you, build it around your business, keep data on your laptops and use plain files, so you can leave any time.
