What we do

Four capabilities that fit together. Most engagements use two or three of them.

◆

Software engineering

Backend services, APIs, data models and the frontends on top of them. Go, Python, JavaScript and React, with the database work taken as seriously as the code that queries it.

◆

Product management

Requirements that a developer can build from and a tester can test from. User stories, functional specs, backlog shaping, and the unglamorous coordination that keeps a release moving.

◆

Quality assurance

Test planning and execution against what was actually specified. Reproducible bug reports, regression coverage, and honest reporting of what is and isn't covered.

◆

AI-assisted systems

Document extraction, classification and retrieval built on current language models — with the validation layer that decides what to trust and what to hold for review.

How we work

Small team, direct contact, no account managers between you and the people building.

  1. We scope before we quote A short conversation about what you're building and where it's stuck, then a written scope. If the work isn't a fit, we say so.
  2. You get hours, not headcount Billing is hourly against logged work, invoiced monthly. You see what was done and how long it took.
  3. Work lands in your repository Your tools, your branch conventions, your review process. Everything we produce is yours, assigned in writing.
  4. Nothing ships unverified Testing isn't a separate phase we bolt on. Whoever builds it says plainly what has been checked and what hasn't.
