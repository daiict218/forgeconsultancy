Privacy policy

Browser Agent (Chrome extension) · Effective 24 October 2026 (updated: no passphrase, memory, history, skills and approval settings) · Publisher: Forge Consultancy, India

In short: Forge Consultancy does not collect, receive or store any of your data. Browser Agent has no server. Your keys, conversation history and memory stay in your browser on your own device. The text you type and the page content the agent reads are sent only to the AI provider you choose, and only when you use the agent.

2. What is sent, and to whom

When you ask the agent to do something, Browser Agent sends a request directly from your browser to the AI provider you selected: OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI or Google. The request contains your message, the text and element labels of the page the agent reads to answer you, and the results of the agent's steps. That provider's own privacy policy and terms apply to those requests.

To answer well, each request also includes the few memories and past-task summaries that match your current message, never your whole history.

To list the models your key can use, Browser Agent asks the provider for its model list. When you choose "Sign in with OpenRouter", the sign-in happens on OpenRouter's website, and OpenRouter returns a key for your account to the extension.

Browser Agent sends nothing to Forge Consultancy and has no analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry.

3. How page content is used

The agent reads a page only when you ask it to work on that page. It uses the content only to carry out your request. It refuses to type into password, card number, card security code and one-time code fields, in every mode. By default, every click, every piece of typing and every page change needs your approval in the panel. You can always allow one type of action on a site you trust; this rule is saved only on your device, and you can reset it in Settings. You can also turn on bypass, which allows all actions without asking until you close the panel.

4. Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The use of information received by Browser Agent adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell user data, do not use or transfer it for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose, and do not use or transfer it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

5. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Side panel: to show the agent next to the page.
  • Storage: to keep your keys, settings and skills on your device.
  • Scripting, active tab and site access: to read the page you ask about and to click or type on it after your approval.
  • Identity: only for "Sign in with OpenRouter", through Chrome's own sign-in window.

6. Your choices

  • Delete all history and memory at any time: Settings, Security, "Forget history and memory".
  • Remove the site permissions you saved at any time: Settings, Security, "Reset site permissions".
  • Delete all keys at any time: Settings, Security, "Delete all keys".
  • Limit the sites the extension can access: chrome://extensions, Browser Agent, Details, Site access.
  • Remove the extension to delete all of its stored data from your browser.

7. Children

Browser Agent is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

8. Changes
